# Research

## Interests
My research focuses on:
- Neuroimaging techniques (e.g., fMRI, DTI).
- Computational modeling to study brain function and behavior.
- Applications of advanced statistical methods in neuroscience.

## Skills
- Programming: Python (NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, Pytorch, matplotlib), R, MATLAB, Github.
- Neuroimaging Tools: FSL, fMRIPrep, Nilearn, Nibabel, Brain Connectivity Toolbox.
- Neuromodulation: TMS/rTMS, SimNIBS (software).
- Statistical Methods: Correlation, Regression, T-tests, ANOVA, P-value analysis.
- Research Methods: Experimental design, Hypothesis testing, Computational modeling.
